Our Story

Sweet Bliss Cakes began as a small family kitchen passion in 2015, born from a love for baking and a desire to spread joy through desserts. Over the years, we’ve grown into a full-service cake studio known for our handcrafted, custom-designed creations that make every occasion extra special.


We believe that a cake should be more than just dessert — it should be an experience. From the first consultation to the final decoration, every step is made with care, creativity, and a sprinkle of love. Our mission is simple: to make your sweetest moments unforgettable.
